ORE EATERS

Dig it out. Haul it home. Hold the line.

Play free on itch.io

A pixel-art asteroid mining game

Start a mining company with one beat-up ship and 5,000 credits. Laser asteroids apart, haul the ore home and sell it, then put your profits into bigger ships, built piece by piece on a grid: bows, hulls, hubs and engines in T, L and cross shapes.

The galaxy doesn't wait for you. Rival factions grow rich and fly ships of their own design. Black Tally pirate gangs demand tolls. Law patrols hunt troublemakers. And an alien swarm is eating its way through the sectors, rock by rock.

In the demo

  • Mine, trade and upgrade in a living galaxy full of radio chatter
  • Build modular ships on a grid. Lose one and it's gone for good
  • Warp gates with fares set by whoever owns the sector
  • Pirates, bounties, law patrols and faction fleets
  • Keyboard or gamepad, free in your browser or on Windows

Play in your browser on itch.io, or download it there for Windows and Mac. The browser version saves in your browser, so use the same browser to continue. It works best in desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
